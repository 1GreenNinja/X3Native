## 1. Premise & Logline

**Logline.** Earth, 2157. A man augmented into a super-soldier by an alien breeding program wakes inside the lab that made him, fights up through seven floors of body-horror to rescue the people he can — including the woman he loves — then carries that war across the galaxy and back to liberate an invaded Earth, every choice rippling toward one of twelve endings.

**The player.** **Jake Hunter**, designated **Subject 7-Alpha**. The alien program enhanced him to **400% strength**, but a **genetic anomaly** let him keep his **free will** (every other subject becomes a loyal hybrid). He wakes on a slab in a detention cell with a monitor reading *"PHASE 1 COMPLETE — Subject 7-Alpha: 400% strength increase achieved,"* discovers his strength by crushing the medical equipment, and escapes ahead of a termination squad. His driving goal: **find Sarah.**

**The lab.** **Lab Zero** — a seven-floor underground facility (revealed late to be **off-world**, on the alien planet **Keth'zar**, not Earth). It is a front for the enemy's human breeding/infection program: humans are enhanced, infected with alien DNA, and **bred to produce a hybrid invasion army** adapted to Earth's atmosphere. Run on-site by the corrupted (and partly remorseful) human scientist **Dr. Chen**.

**The antagonist(s).**
- **The Overlord** — not a single being but a **collective hive consciousness spanning thousands of conquered worlds** (the bible escalates the count from "47 worlds" → "2,847 worlds" by the finale). It conquers via *gifts* — technology and medicine — then infection, then breeding. Final boss is a 4-phase confrontation.
- **Dr. Chen** — the on-site architect of the horror; an Act-1 boss and/or redeemable ally.
- **Jake's Clone** — a cold, "optimized" duplicate loyal to the Overlord; the **Act-1 climax boss** on Floor 7, found assaulting Sarah.

**The stakes.** Earth has roughly **72 hours** (per Chen / K'thara) before the invasion fleet arrives; the bible later treats the invasion as already underway by the time Jake returns. Personally: saving Sarah and the captives before their **transformation timers** expire. Thematically: **preserving human autonomy, identity, and dignity** against a force that sees people as breeding stock.

## 2. Story Spine by Act

EFLZ is a **50-level / 4-act** campaign in the canonical bible. (An engine-side reimagining stretches this to 100 levels / 3 acts — see §7.)

### ACT 1 — Lab Zero, the Facility (Floors / Levels 1–7)
Psychological/body horror; the choices here lock the timeline for the rest of the game.
- **Floor 1 — Detention.** Wake as 7-Alpha, discover strength, escape the cell. **Boss: Security Chief Martinez** (cybernetic ex-military who believes he's guarding legitimate research; dies learning the truth). Find pistol + bazooka.
- **Floor 2 — Medical Bay.** The signature beat: **three simultaneous timed rescues** (Aria / Keisha / Emily) plus Sarah's distant Floor-7 timer. You can only reach 1–2 in time. **Boss: Dr. Chen (corrupted)** — 3-phase degeneration; begs for death. Introduces the infection timer and breeding chambers.
- **Floor 3 — Genetics Lab.** Hybrid specimens in tanks, breeding pods (destroy or face infinite spawns), heartbreaking environmental storytelling. **Boss: Failed Experiment #7** — Jake's tragic predecessor who retained enough humanity to suffer ("Kill me before I forget her name!").
- **Floor 4 — Cybernetics Workshop.** Optional **augmentations cost "Humanity"**; too many lock out good endings. **Boss: The Collective / The Chorus** (five scientists merged into one cyber-organism). Unlocks **Chaingun**.
- **Floor 5 — Drone Manufacturing (DroneStation).** Wireframe drone swarms; hackable. **Sarah's 90-second master hack** (on the later return mission) converts the entire drone network to allies. **Boss: Swarm Controller AI**. Unlocks **Plasma Rifle**.
- **Floor 6 — Alien Technology.** First contact with the **Salvari** refugees and **K'thara**; learn the **cure**; the invasion's true scope is revealed. **Boss: Alien Overseer**. Unlocks **Lightning Gun** (if Salvari allied).
- **Floor 7 — Executive Laboratory (the Clone Crisis).** Jake finds **Sarah restrained** with **Jake's Clone** assaulting her against a **7–9 minute genetic-recombination timer**. **Boss: Jake's Clone** (3 phases: mirror-match → nanite boost → activates backup clones). **The timeline (Alpha/Beta/Omega/Gamma) locks here.** A **Return Mission** then descends through hidden sub-levels (Waste Disposal, Cryo Storage, Enhanced Interrogation) to **rescue Dr. Chen** from a regeneration-torture device — saving him yields the perfect cure and full invasion intel.

### ACT 2 — Alien World / Open World (Levels 8–20)
Escape onto the surface; discovery, alliance-building, learning the enemy. Setting: the **Keth'zar system** (Salvari homeworld region).
- **L8 Surface Emergence** — step into alien daylight (twin suns, purple sky); meet Salvari scout **R'thek**.
- **L9–11 Crystalline Desert** — the **Salvari Archives** (history of conquered worlds, cure formulas, Crystal Heart shield plans — pick only 3 databases); the **Hidden Oasis** forward base where **K'thara joins permanently** and awakens a **200,000-strong Salvari colony fleet**; the **Spaceport Ruins** where the team steals K'thara's ship **Storm Runner** (boss: **Overlord Sentinel**). Act 2 effectively ends here in the master design (Lvl 11 = "Act 2 Complete," 22%); other docs extend Act 2 through the **Advanced Cave System (Lvl 12, Crystal Heart chamber, boss Memory Hunter)**, **Toxic Swamplands**, **Ruined Metropolis**, and a final **Spaceport** departure at Lvl 20.
- **Key turn:** the Salvari are the cautionary mirror — *"thirty survive from thirty billion"*; their world fell in five months. Their warning ("no negotiation, no gifts, fight from day one") sets the strategy for Earth.

### ACT 3 — Space Journey (Levels 21–35)
Race home aboard the **Storm Runner** (AI: *Seeker*); recruit allies, build a fleet, deepen relationships.
- **L21–22** first spaceflight + FTL jump tutorial; fuel mining; harmless **Void Rays**.
- **L23–25 Salvari Prime ruins** — K'thara's devastated true homeworld; archives reveal the Overlord's full nature; boss **Memory Hunter** in the Memorial City.
- **L26–28 Mining Colony Liberation** — free enslaved workers; recruit specialists.
- **L29–30 Space Casino "Fortune's End"** — espionage + gambling for the location of the Earth invasion fleet.
- **L31–33 Asteroid Rebel Base** — multi-species alliance; fleet assembly; optional **marriage ceremony** (Omega).
- **L34–35 Jupiter Moon Base / Sol Arrival** — Earth's outer perimeter; discover the **invasion already in progress** (~20M dead and rising); choose a landing zone.

### ACT 4 — Earth's Last Stand (Levels 36–50)
Liberation campaign; deploy all accumulated allies; converge on the Overlord.
- **L36–38** orbital assault (boss **Overlord Dreadnought**) → fiery atmospheric entry → first resistance base; ~20% of humanity already converted.
- **L39–44** **Regional Liberation** (choose order: NA / Europe / Asia / Africa / S. America / Australia) → **Major City Reclamation** (New York, London, Tokyo, Moscow, Cairo, São Paulo).
- **L45–47** the **Global Counter-Offensive** — all allies attack at once; Salvari fleet + hacked drone armies decisive if earned. (Some engine docs insert a **"Return to Lab Zero"** full-circle level here.)
- **L48–49** **Overlord Mothership** infiltration; **Proto-Overlord** boss rush of corrupted Earth leaders (+ in Timeline Beta, the transformed women's finale).
- **L50 The Final Confrontation** — the true Overlord, a **collective consciousness across thousands of worlds**; a **4-phase finale (Avatar → Swarm → Truth → Choice)** that branches into the **12 endings**.

## 4. The Morality / Rescue-Triage System

The defining mechanic. On **Floor 2**, three captives (Aria 5:00 / Keisha 7:00 / Emily 4:00 in the cutscene script — `Floor2_MedicalBayController.cs` uses much larger placeholder values 15/20/9 min) are each on a **live transformation countdown**, while **Sarah** runs a long background timer up on Floor 7 (15:00). With 400% speed you still **cannot reach everyone** on a first run, forcing prioritization under pressure.

**The core motif: "the unsaved becomes a boss."** Each failed rescue is not just a death — the victim **transforms and returns as a recurring boss** later in the game, voiced with the memory of the moment Jake passed their door:
- **Aria → The Siren** ("The Voice That Breaks Hearts") — sonic/psychic; *"I screamed your name. You passed my door. You chose her."*
- **Keisha → Breeder Queen** ("The Warrior Who Became Monster") — swarm commander; *"She held on. She fought. WHERE WERE YOU?"*
- **Emily → The Oracle** ("The Mind That Became Weapon") — psychic tactician; *"You could have saved all four with 87% probability... you simply chose."*

Each transformed woman can later be **killed** or, with the late-game cure, **separated/cured** into a damaged but living ally (a partial-redemption questline).

**The triage outcome chooses a TIMELINE** that recolors the rest of the 50 levels (this is the central branching spine, locked at Floor 7):

| Timeline | Trigger (Floor 2–7 execution) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **OMEGA — "The Perfect Rescue"** | Perfect run: all three women saved with time to spare **and** Sarah saved (total <14:30). ~10% first-playthrough. | Jake + **Sarah** (married); the others survive as **found-family friends** (each pairs off). Most hopeful; standard bosses only. |
| **ALPHA — "The Grief That Built a Family"** | Good run: 2–3 women saved, but **Sarah lost/mercy-killed** at Floor 7. ~65% first-playthrough. | Sarah dies/transforms; **polyamorous family** forms with Aria/Keisha/Emily. Bittersweet, hopeful. |
| **BETA — "The Cost of Love"** | Player **beelines to Floor 7** to save Sarah first → the three women's timers expire. ~25%. | Sarah saved, but Aria/Keisha/Emily become **The Siren / Breeder Queen / The Oracle** recurring bosses; Sarah **leaves from guilt** (Year 3); Jake fights solo, K'thara romance opens. Darkest/most tragic. |
| **GAMMA — "Sarah Lost"** | **Fail to reach Sarah** before 100% completion. | Sarah fully transforms into **"The Bride"** (kills the Clone herself); drives the Nightmare/total-failure track. |

Wider **morality axes** are tracked separately and feed the endings: **Humanity** (save vs abandon), **Trust** (ally vs attack the Salvari), **Mercy** (kill vs cure infected), **Love** (support vs abandon Sarah), **Redemption** (save vs kill Dr. Chen), and the **Augmentation/"Humanity score"** (too many cybernetic augments on Floor 4 lock out good endings and push toward the corruption path). 500+ variables are tracked overall.

## 5. The 12 Endings

Determined by timeline, surviving-ally count, Salvari alliance, Chen's fate, augmentation level, and finale choice. Endings 1–7 are fully scripted in `TASK_7_..._DIALOGUE_SCRIPTS.md` (Part VII); 8–12 are summarized there.

| # | Ending | Branch conditions (triggers) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Golden (Perfect)** | Timeline **Omega** + **all** allies + **Salvari alliance** + **Chen's sacrifice** + Overlord destroyed. Earth heals; Jake & Sarah and family; humanity becomes an alliance. |
| 2 | **Good (Strong Victory)** | Timeline **Alpha/Omega** + **most** allies + Salvari alliance + Overlord destroyed (one ally — Keisha/Aria/Emily — fell). Earth saved but scarred. |
| 3 | **Bittersweet (Pyrrhic)** | Timeline **Alpha** + **some allies lost** + Salvari alliance + Overlord destroyed at great cost. Earth free but devastated; billions dead. |
| 4 | **Tragic (Sarah's Sacrifice)** | Timeline **Omega** + **high relationship** + **Sarah sacrifices herself** to deliver the virus into the Overlord network. Earth saved; Jake grieves (K'thara consoles). |
| 5 | **Fractured (Relationship Failure)** | Timeline **Alpha**, **relationship strained** — team survives but the family **breaks apart**; Jake isolated, K'thara pulls him back. |
| 6 | **Dark (Jake Corrupted)** | **Failed most rescues** + minimal allies + **Jake absorbs Overlord tech/power** (high augmentation). Jake becomes a controlling "guardian/god"; K'thara resists. |
| 7 | **Nightmare (Total Failure)** | Timeline **Gamma** + **Sarah transformed (The Bride)** + **no allies** + **Overlord victory**. Earth converted; Jake kept conscious as breeding overseer. |
| 8 | **Solo Victory** | Jake **alone** defeats the Overlord but **loses everyone**. Bittersweet survival. |
| 9 | **K'thara Romance** | **Timeline Beta** path; **K'thara & Jake together**, rebuilding with the Salvari. |
| 10 | **Polyamorous Family** | **Timeline Alpha**; all relationships maintained; the **found family thrives**. |
| 11 | **Chen's Redemption** | **Chen survives** (alternate method) and devotes his life to **curing the infection** worldwide. |
| 12 | **New Beginning** | **Minimum victory conditions**; Jake + **one ally** start fresh on a new world. |

> Cross-doc note: the v4.0 bible also names flavor endings — **Wedding/Golden**, **Emperor (corruption)**, **Sacrifice (hero)**, plus tier labels (Golden/Good/Neutral/Bad/Nightmare). These map onto the 12 above (Wedding≈1, Emperor≈6, Sacrifice≈4/8). The 12-ending screenplay in TASK_7 is the most complete/latest enumeration and is treated as canonical here.

## 6. Key Locations & Motifs

- **Lab Zero / "The Spire."** The seven-floor facility (engine docs render it as a vertical tower with a 7-stop elevator). Each floor a horror set-piece (detention → medical → genetics → cybernetics → drones → alien tech → executive). The big reveal: it sits **on the alien world Keth'zar, not Earth.**
- **Club 1127.** A **hidden neon hub** reached via the elevator/keypad code **1127** — fixer NPC, music, contraband upgrades, side-quests, a "PlasmaGlobe" mechanic. **Note:** this is an *engine-side* canon element (`MASTER_GAME_PLAN.md`, derived from the shipped web game + novel), **not present in the 50-level bible.** Persistent hub.
- **The Mirror level (L48 "The Mirror").** A no-combat **corridor of mirrors** that rejects the false binary "sacrifice yourself / sacrifice your friends" — the player **breaks the central mirror** to find a hidden **third path** (K'thara: *"come back and teach the rest of us"*). This was the **original web game's ending**, repositioned as Act-2's climax in the engine plan. (In the bible's own 50-level numbering, L48 is instead the Overlord-Mothership assault — see §7.)
- **Crystal / Crystalline Desert & the Advanced Cave System (Lvl 12).** Bioluminescent caves, underground oasis/base, **ancient ruins predating the Overlord**, and the **Crystal Heart chamber** — a planetary-defense power source (and the basis of Earth's defensive **Crystal Heart shield**).
- **Storm Runner.** The Salvari ship (AI *Seeker*) acquired at the end of Act 2 — the **Act-3 hub** for crew, upgrades, and relationship scenes.
- **Salvari Archives / Memorial Wall.** Repositories of the genocided Salvari's history (the "30 from 30 billion" motif) — the cautionary mirror for Earth.
- **The Breeding Chambers / Executive Lab.** The recurring horror motif: **forced breeding as the ultimate violation** (loss of autonomy and identity), embodied by the Floor-7 Clone/Sarah crisis.
- **Recurring motifs:** the **transformation timer** (humanity lost in real time), **"everyone counts / no one is expendable,"** environmental storytelling (children's drawings, love letters, video logs), and **the false binary broken by a third choice** (the Mirror).

## 7. Reconciliation Note — Engine Encoding vs. Fuller Narrative

The X3Native engine currently encodes only the **opening slice of Act 1** plus high-level plans. Two design visions also diverge and must be tracked:

**What the engine currently encodes / plans (concrete):**
- **Floor 1: Chief Martinez boss** (per implemented `Floor1_AwakeningManager.cs`) — *see conflict below*.
- **Floor 2: the 3-victim timed rescue** (Aria/Keisha/Emily) with the "unsaved → boss" motif (Siren/Breeder Queen/Oracle).
- **Floor 7: Jake's Clone boss + Sarah rescue** against the recombination timer; timeline lock.
- **Club 1127** (code 1127) and the **deep tunnels** as secret hubs; the **L48 Mirror** room; **Salvari/K'thara** alliance; factions **Dominion / Verthani / Illuminated / Salvari**.

**Biggest narrative beats NOT yet encoded (flag for build-out):**
1. **The Return Mission & Dr. Chen's rescue** — the entire post-Clone descent through hidden sub-levels (Waste Disposal / Cryo Storage / Enhanced Interrogation) and **Sarah's 90-second drone-network master hack**. This is the hinge that determines cure quality and invasion intel; currently absent.
2. **Floors 3–6 bosses & systems** — Failed Experiment #7, The Collective/Chorus + the **augmentation/"Humanity score"**, the Swarm Controller AI, the Alien Overseer + Salvari cure path. Only F1/F2/F7 are represented.
3. **The four-way timeline system (Omega/Alpha/Beta/Gamma)** and its long-tail consequences (Sarah's departure, the transformed-women boss encounters across Acts 2–4, the polyamorous vs. monogamous vs. solo family structures). The engine notes timeline lock at F7 but none of the downstream content.
4. **Acts 3–4 in full** — the Storm Runner space hub, fleet/alliance recruitment, Earth regional/city liberation, the Overlord Mothership, and the **4-phase finale → 12 endings**.

**Structural conflicts to resolve (do not silently pick one):**
- **50-level/4-act bible vs. 100-level/3-act engine plan.** `MASTER_GAME_PLAN.md` reimagines the game as **100 levels in 3 acts** (Spire 1–14 → Alien World 15–48 → Resistance/Beyond 49–100), folding the bible's Acts 3–4 into "Act 3 — Beyond" and treating the **L48 Mirror as Act-2's climax**. The bible's L48 is the **Overlord Mothership assault** and L50 is the finale. These are two different games sharing a story; the engine plan is the *current building target*, the bible is the *narrative source of truth*.
- **Martinez boss placement.** Implemented `Floor1_AwakeningManager.cs` + the bestiary put **Martinez on Floor 1** (Chen = Floor 2 boss). `TASK_9_MISSING_SCRIPTS_SPEC.md` re-specs Floor 1 with **no boss** (a Chen *trust choice*) and moves Martinez to Floor 2. **Follow the implemented version (Martinez on Floor 1)** and flag.
- **Rescue-timer values disagree** (cutscene 5/7/4 min vs. controller 15/20/9 + Sarah 30). Treat as tuning placeholders.
- **Earth's countdown** is "72 hours" in the bible's Act-1 dialogue but "invasion already underway / main fleet in 60 days" by Act 3. Treat as escalating in-fiction urgency, not a continuity bug to "fix."
- **A fourth timeline ("Gamma," Sarah fully lost → The Bride)** appears in the dialogue scripts but is folded into "Alpha/Beta" in the master design's three-timeline framing. Canonical for the *Nightmare* ending; treat Gamma as the failure-state extension of the triage system.
