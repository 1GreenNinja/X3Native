## 2. THE ENGINE'S REAL CONTRACT (verified by two audits + A/B captures)

**X3Native is GPU-DRIVEN.** `drawMesh*` records **no** GPU commands — it appends a CPU
`DrawRecord`; `endFrame()` emits the whole frame as **multidraw-indirect** (bindless + per-object
SSBO). "CPU-deferred" describes only *when commands are written*, never where work runs.

1. **Per-frame STATE setters are ORDER-FREE.** `setPointLights`, `setCamera`, `setSkyParams`,
   `setAmbient`, `setFog`, `setGrade`, `setExposure`, `setPostFX`, `setShadowBounds`,
   `setWaterParams`, `setDebugView`, RT/GI/IBL params — all read **once** in
   `endFrame() → prepareFrameData()` into one frame-global UBO every draw samples. Position vs
   `beginFrame`/draws is **bit-identical**.
2. **Only two latch at `beginFrame`:** `setVsync`, `resize`.
3. **`registerTerrainMaterial` is the ONLY must-precede-the-draws call** (baked per `DrawRecord`).
4. **⚠ THE INVERSE IS A REAL BUG:** `drawMesh*`, `drawHud*`, `submitParticles`, `submitDecals` are
   **CLEARED BY `beginFrame`** — they must come AFTER it. **Never hoist a draw.**
5. **Known hazards (documented, unfixed):** `setPointLights` silently truncates at **64** lights ·
   `setPostFX` is **edge-triggered** (two calls/frame can swallow an AE snap or TAA invalidate) ·
   `setShadowBounds` sets a **one-way latch** pinning the shadow box process-wide ·
   `worldToScreen` returns a **one-frame-stale** viewProj.

**Debug views** (`ECHO_DEBUGVIEW=N`): 1 normals · 2 point-light term · 3 albedo · 4 ambient/IBL ·
5 grey-albedo. **Use these before theorising.**

## 3. THE MISDIAGNOSIS — READ THIS BEFORE CLAIMING A FIX

Night alley was black. I saw "posts glow, street stays black", concluded `setPointLights` ran
mid-frame, changed ordering **and** lamp range in ONE commit (`10d367b`), saw a brighter frame,
declared victory, and **broadcast it to the fleet.**

Both changes were **no-ops**, proven by A/B capture:
- hoisted vs mid-frame `setPointLights` → **max channel diff 9/255** (TAA dither)
- lamp range 17 m vs 54 m → **max channel diff 8/255**

The real fix was **`e49b91a` — a PARALLEL LANE's commit** that landed between my before-capture and
my rebuild: night exposure lift + ambient floor + harvesting ~60 **emissive-only** neon/lamp sources
into **real PointLights**. They had been self-glowing while illuminating nothing.

**Rules this bought us:**
- **In a multi-lane repo, `git log` between your before/after captures.** Another agent's commit
  lands in your rebuild and looks like your fix. This is *the* trap.
- **One commit, two hunks → name the one you PROVED.** A two-capture A/B takes ~90 seconds.
- **Don't broadcast a causal story you haven't isolated.** A confident wrong lesson planted in
  comments + chat is worse than silence — the next agent inherits it as fact.
- **A plausible mechanism that explains the symptom is not evidence.** This one explained it
  perfectly and was still false.
- **Emissive ≠ light transport.** An emissive material illuminates *nothing*. This is the single
  most useful lighting lesson in the project.

## 6. KEY GOTCHAS (hard-won, do not relearn)

- **Coordinates:** engine is RH **Y-up**, +X east, +Z north; yaw 0 = +X, π/2 = +Z. Matrices are
  **column-major float[16]**. Unity → engine needs a **Z mirror**; Blender is **Z-up** (author ground
  quads in Blender X-Y at Z=height). Facing north puts **west on screen-right** — top-down captures
  read mirrored. Full law: `docs/COORDINATES.md`.
- **Crown ground is Y ≈ 190**, not 0 — "street level" cameras need y ≈ 200-240.
- **The Unity Asset Store cache is the master key:** every purchased pack's pristine
  `.unitypackage` lives in `C:\Users\Tim\AppData\Roaming\Unity\Asset Store-5.x\<Publisher>\`
  (gzip tar). The extracted packs on `D:\Assets` are **lossy** — original demo scenes, `.mat`s and
  `.meta`s live only in the package.
- **Leartes packs ship EMPTY placeholder `.mat`s** (dead GUID chain) → `tools/leartes_reskin.py`
  heals textures by name-family matching.
- **HDRP kit meshes are mixed-winding** → the mesh pipeline is **cull NONE + `gl_FrontFacing`
  normal flip**; back-culling rendered them as hollow black shells.
- **Prefab expansion must filter LOD1-3** (Unity prefabs stack all four LOD copies) and be
  **all-or-nothing** (a partial expansion silently drops signs/gates).
- **Build:** `cmake --build --preset windows-vs2026 --parallel 4` — the 14900K is **degraded**
  (confirmed WHEA parity errors), so always wrap in a 5× retry loop; transient failures are normal.
- **Capture:** `--shot-cam "x,y,z,yaw,pitch" --screenshot out.png N`. Env: `ECHO_TOD=golden|night|
  dusk|noon`, `ECHO_RESIDENTS=1`, `ECHO_PANEL=1`, `ECHO_DEBUGVIEW=N`, `ECHO_RT=1`,
  `ECHO_LAMP_RANGE_MUL` / `ECHO_LAMP_INT_MUL`.
- **Meshy premium** is wired: `tools/meshy_gen.py` (key at
  `//p13700/g/X3Native/fleet-handoff/meshy-key.txt`) — text → preview → refine → textured GLB.

## 8. VOLUMETRIC ROADMAP — closing the gap to UE5 (added 2026-07-25)

**Why our night aerial doesn't glow:** we raymarch PER-PIXEL from camera to scene depth, and the
forward light set is 64 **camera-local** lamps with 9-15 m ranges. At 700 m altitude the march ends
above the city and nothing is near enough to scatter. Architecture gap, not a bug.

**How UE5/Witcher does it:** a **FROXEL VOLUME** — a 3D texture of frustum voxels (~160x90x64) over
the whole frustum. (1) every light **injects** into the volume via compute, so a distant light still
writes to the froxels it touches; (2) **temporal reprojection** makes it stable and cheap at 4K;
(3) a **Sky Atmosphere LUT** handles long-range aerial perspective separately; (4) **Lumen** makes
emissive surfaces emit into GI, so neon lights the fog AND the wall at any distance.

**Plan, value ÷ effort:**
1. **Enable DDGI** (config) — bounced light; probes also carry glow outward. Never called in this world.
2. **District glow proxies** (small, host-only) — cluster each district's ~60 neon sources into ~3
   big low-intensity lights, range 200-400 m, appended to `districtLights`. Gives the existing
   volumetric something to scatter from altitude → city glow dome. **Best value/effort; do first
   after DDGI.**
3. **Froxel volume** (large) — 3D texture + compute light injection + temporal reproject, replacing
   the per-pixel march. Fixes distance AND cost permanently. The real UE5 architecture.
4. **Height fog + aerial-perspective LUT** (medium) — fog pools in the fjord; physical long-range.
5. **Cascaded shadow maps** (medium) — still a single ortho box; needed for shafts to read at all
   distances.

**Also unfixed, cheap, known:** SSR ray-march distance is HARDCODED to 48 m
(`vk_passes.cpp:517`) — promote to `r_ssr_maxdist` so puddles reflect neon across wide boulevards.
Volumetric perf is UNPROFILED (48 steps x (1 shadow tap + <=24 lights) at full res) and temporal
stability under motion is untested (stills only).

2. **`lightFixtures()` never fed to `setPointLights`** for hall regions — API exists, call may not.
3. **Nearest-64 selection starves them** — hall fixtures not in the selected set.

## 11. ⭐ SUSPECTED ROOT CAUSE: COLOR-SPACE / AO STACKING (Tim, 2026-07-25) — INVESTIGATE FIRST

**Tim's report (source outside this session — NOT in the last 60 fleet messages, follow up with
@djbooth / Icebox Garage):** "the lighting was gamma numbers translating to another form of lighting
numbers — dark was too dark and light was too light. Also SSAO and the other methods conflicted."

**Why this outranks everything in §5 and §8:** "dark too dark AND light too light IN THE SAME FRAME"
is the classic signature of a **COLOR-SPACE MISMATCH** — sRGB values treated as linear, or the ~2.2
transfer curve applied twice. It collapses mid-tones toward both extremes. **Our own AAA art review
independently reported exactly this**, twice: *"crushed-black geometry under a blown-white sky"* and
*"the exposure/ambient/GI chain is not functioning."*

**IMPLICATION: every ambient floor / exposure lift / night fill tuned in this session may be
COMPENSATION FOR A BENT CURVE, not a real fix.** That would explain why each lighting fix kept
half-working. Do NOT tune further until this is settled.

**Check A — color space:**
- `ModelLoader.cpp`: baseColor + emissive resolve with `srgb=true`; normal/MR/occlusion with
  `srgb=false`. Verify that is actually honoured at upload (VK_FORMAT_*_SRGB vs _UNORM) and that
  nothing double-converts in `composite.frag` (ACES tonemap + grade) or at swapchain present.
- Suspect list: swapchain format sRGB vs manual pow(1/2.2); HDR target format; the WebP/Draco armory
  textures; `bakeMouthGlowRGBA` and other hand-baked textures (authored in which space?).

**Check B — AO stacking:** we run SSAO + RTAO + DDGI + IBL occlusion simultaneously. `mesh.frag`
multiplies `ao` into the diffuse ambient (`:578`), the ambient-specular (`:580`) and the IBL path
(`:673`, with a separate `specAo`). Determine whether RTAO and SSAO write the SAME `ssaoTex` (fine)
or apply independently (double/triple darkening). Also check DDGI isn't darkening a third time.

**Instrument:** `ECHO_DEBUGVIEW=3` (albedo alone) and `=4` (ambient/IBL alone) — if albedo reads
correct mid-grey but the beauty pass is bimodal, the curve is the problem, not the materials.

### §11 UPDATE — ✅ CONFIRMED BY @djbooth: **LINEAR vs GAMMA**

Tim relayed DJBooth's confirmation: the lighting problem is a **linear/gamma (color-space)
mismatch**, exactly matching the "dark too dark AND light too light" signature and our art review's
"crushed blacks under a blown-white sky." **This is THE root cause. Fix it before any further
lighting tuning** — several of this session's ambient/exposure changes are compensation for it and
should be RE-EVALUATED (likely reverted/retuned) once the curve is correct.

**THE RULE (Vulkan):**
- **COLOR textures** (baseColor, emissive) → upload as `VK_FORMAT_*_SRGB` so the GPU linearises on
  sample.
- **DATA textures** (normal, metallicRoughness, AO, masks, heightfields) → `*_UNORM`, never sRGB.
- **All lighting math in LINEAR.**
- **Convert to sRGB exactly ONCE at the end** — either an sRGB swapchain format OR a manual
  `pow(1/2.2)` in composite, **never both**. Double-conversion washes/blows highlights; a missing
  conversion crushes darks. Getting BOTH symptoms at once means the two ends disagree.

**PRIME SUSPECTS in this repo — hand-baked textures authored this session/lane, check their upload
format matches their authored space:**
- `assets/infra/road_tile.png` / `road_asphalt.glb` (albedo — sRGB-authored via PIL)
- the puddle **metallicRoughness** map (DATA — must be UNORM/linear; if uploaded sRGB it is wrong)
- `assets/interiors/*.glb` emissive room colours (PIL-authored)
- `mine_fx.cpp` `bakeMouthGlowRGBA` (hand-baked RGBA)
- `tools/leartes_reskin.py` injected baseColor/normal/MR (does it tag color vs data correctly?)
- `tools/inject_emissive.py` embedded emissive PNGs
- the armory's WebP/Draco textures

**Verify:** `ECHO_DEBUGVIEW=3` (albedo alone) — a correct mid-grey albedo under a bimodal beauty pass
proves the curve, not the materials. Then re-shoot the AAA review set and re-tune ambient/exposure
FROM SCRATCH.
