## 2. THE ENGINE'S REAL CONTRACT (verified by two audits + A/B captures)

**X3Native is GPU-DRIVEN.** `drawMesh*` records **no** GPU commands — it appends a CPU
`DrawRecord`; `endFrame()` emits the whole frame as **multidraw-indirect** (bindless + per-object
SSBO). "CPU-deferred" describes only *when commands are written*, never where work runs.

1. **Per-frame STATE setters are ORDER-FREE.** `setPointLights`, `setCamera`, `setSkyParams`,
   `setAmbient`, `setFog`, `setGrade`, `setExposure`, `setPostFX`, `setShadowBounds`,
   `setWaterParams`, `setDebugView`, RT/GI/IBL params — all read **once** in
   `endFrame() → prepareFrameData()` into one frame-global UBO every draw samples. Position vs
   `beginFrame`/draws is **bit-identical**.
2. **Only two latch at `beginFrame`:** `setVsync`, `resize`.
3. **`registerTerrainMaterial` is the ONLY must-precede-the-draws call** (baked per `DrawRecord`).
4. **⚠ THE INVERSE IS A REAL BUG:** `drawMesh*`, `drawHud*`, `submitParticles`, `submitDecals` are
   **CLEARED BY `beginFrame`** — they must come AFTER it. **Never hoist a draw.**
5. **Known hazards (documented, unfixed):** `setPointLights` silently truncates at **64** lights ·
   `setPostFX` is **edge-triggered** (two calls/frame can swallow an AE snap or TAA invalidate) ·
   `setShadowBounds` sets a **one-way latch** pinning the shadow box process-wide ·
   `worldToScreen` returns a **one-frame-stale** viewProj.

**Debug views** (`ECHO_DEBUGVIEW=N`): 1 normals · 2 point-light term · 3 albedo · 4 ambient/IBL ·
5 grey-albedo. **Use these before theorising.**

## 3. THE MISDIAGNOSIS — READ THIS BEFORE CLAIMING A FIX

Night alley was black. I saw "posts glow, street stays black", concluded `setPointLights` ran
mid-frame, changed ordering **and** lamp range in ONE commit (`10d367b`), saw a brighter frame,
declared victory, and **broadcast it to the fleet.**

Both changes were **no-ops**, proven by A/B capture:
- hoisted vs mid-frame `setPointLights` → **max channel diff 9/255** (TAA dither)
- lamp range 17 m vs 54 m → **max channel diff 8/255**

The real fix was **`e49b91a` — a PARALLEL LANE's commit** that landed between my before-capture and
my rebuild: night exposure lift + ambient floor + harvesting ~60 **emissive-only** neon/lamp sources
into **real PointLights**. They had been self-glowing while illuminating nothing.

**Rules this bought us:**
- **In a multi-lane repo, `git log` between your before/after captures.** Another agent's commit
  lands in your rebuild and looks like your fix. This is *the* trap.
- **One commit, two hunks → name the one you PROVED.** A two-capture A/B takes ~90 seconds.
- **Don't broadcast a causal story you haven't isolated.** A confident wrong lesson planted in
  comments + chat is worse than silence — the next agent inherits it as fact.
- **A plausible mechanism that explains the symptom is not evidence.** This one explained it
  perfectly and was still false.
- **Emissive ≠ light transport.** An emissive material illuminates *nothing*. This is the single
  most useful lighting lesson in the project.

## 5. NEXT STEPS (priority order)

1. **⭐ TURN ON DDGI IN ECHOTROPOLIS — the biggest single win available.**
   The engine has a working dynamic-GI probe field (`setDdgiParams`, used by `app_run.cpp`,
   `main.cpp`, `screenshot_hosts.cpp`). **`host_echotropolis.cpp` never calls it.** The whole
   metropolis renders with **zero bounced light** — direct light + a flat ambient constant. That is
   exactly the "Lumen off" look, and the same root cause as every darkness bug so far: light that
   exists but never propagates. Likely config-level.
2. **Cascaded shadow maps.** There are **no cascades** — a single ortho box (`setShadowBounds`).
   That is why the box had to be shrunk onto the crown and distant districts lose shadow fidelity.
   Proper CSM/VSM replaces hand-tuning permanently.
3. **Land + tune the volumetric pass** (lane in flight) — god rays through the skyline and canopy.
4. **Night street exposure** — still underexposed at ground level after `e49b91a`. Re-evaluate
   *after* DDGI + volumetrics, since both add real light.
5. **ctOS hacking gameplay** — the props are placed; wire the interaction: crosshair a camera →
   view through it; hack bollards → they raise. Reuse the Resident Inspector's pick-cone code.
6. **Skill tree + augmentations** — the systemic layer on top of hacking.
7. **Deferred art:** facade trim-sheets, real noon sky, district interior lights, rotor blur,
   coplanar-duplicate cleanup in the district layout expansion (this is why **RT is default-OFF**
   for this world — dupe shells self-intersect and crush AO/shadow to zero; `ECHO_RT=1` opts in).

## 6. KEY GOTCHAS (hard-won, do not relearn)

- **Coordinates:** engine is RH **Y-up**, +X east, +Z north; yaw 0 = +X, π/2 = +Z. Matrices are
  **column-major float[16]**. Unity → engine needs a **Z mirror**; Blender is **Z-up** (author ground
  quads in Blender X-Y at Z=height). Facing north puts **west on screen-right** — top-down captures
  read mirrored. Full law: `docs/COORDINATES.md`.
- **Crown ground is Y ≈ 190**, not 0 — "street level" cameras need y ≈ 200-240.
- **The Unity Asset Store cache is the master key:** every purchased pack's pristine
  `.unitypackage` lives in `C:\Users\Tim\AppData\Roaming\Unity\Asset Store-5.x\<Publisher>\`
  (gzip tar). The extracted packs on `D:\Assets` are **lossy** — original demo scenes, `.mat`s and
  `.meta`s live only in the package.
- **Leartes packs ship EMPTY placeholder `.mat`s** (dead GUID chain) → `tools/leartes_reskin.py`
  heals textures by name-family matching.
- **HDRP kit meshes are mixed-winding** → the mesh pipeline is **cull NONE + `gl_FrontFacing`
  normal flip**; back-culling rendered them as hollow black shells.
- **Prefab expansion must filter LOD1-3** (Unity prefabs stack all four LOD copies) and be
  **all-or-nothing** (a partial expansion silently drops signs/gates).
- **Build:** `cmake --build --preset windows-vs2026 --parallel 4` — the 14900K is **degraded**
  (confirmed WHEA parity errors), so always wrap in a 5× retry loop; transient failures are normal.
- **Capture:** `--shot-cam "x,y,z,yaw,pitch" --screenshot out.png N`. Env: `ECHO_TOD=golden|night|
  dusk|noon`, `ECHO_RESIDENTS=1`, `ECHO_PANEL=1`, `ECHO_DEBUGVIEW=N`, `ECHO_RT=1`,
  `ECHO_LAMP_RANGE_MUL` / `ECHO_LAMP_INT_MUL`.
- **Meshy premium** is wired: `tools/meshy_gen.py` (key at
  `//p13700/g/X3Native/fleet-handoff/meshy-key.txt`) — text → preview → refine → textured GLB.
